# Content Scout Profile

Fill this in once, save it somewhere durable (e.g.
`~/.hermes/content-scout/profile.md`), and point the skill at it. Everything
below is an example — replace it with your own.

## Channel / brand

- Name:
- Where you publish (YouTube, X, TikTok, newsletter, etc.):

## Niches (comma-separated, be specific)

e.g. `swing trading setups, no-code AI automation, solo business operations`

## Audience

Who's watching and why. One or two sentences — their skill level, what they
want out of your content, what they already know so you don't re-explain it.

## Formats you actually make

List only what you can act on. e.g. `12-15 min long-form YouTube`,
`60s Shorts`, `X threads`, `live screen-share walkthroughs`.

## Voice / tone

A few adjectives, or "sounds like [reference creator]". Used to keep hook
suggestions in your register instead of generic clickbait.

## Competitors / channels to watch

List 5-10 accounts/channels in your space. The scout checks these to score
Competitive Gap — has this exact angle already been done to death by them?

## Topics to avoid

Anything off-limits: legal risk, past controversies, saturated formats
you're sick of, or niches adjacent to yours you don't want to drift into.

## Freshness window

How long a "why now" claim stays valid for you. e.g. `72h` for
newsjack-style content, `30d` for slower-moving evergreen niches.

## Cutoff / cadence

- Minimum composite score to brief (default 7.0/10, raise it if you want
  fewer, stronger ideas)
- How many finalists per run (default 5)
- Opportunities log path (e.g. `~/.hermes/content-scout/log.jsonl`)
- Outcomes log path (e.g. `~/.hermes/content-scout/outcomes.jsonl`) — see
  `references/outcomes-log-format.md`. Optional but this is what lets the
  skill calibrate toward what's actually worked instead of starting from
  scratch every run.
